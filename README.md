## What We Do

We build AI that understands your musical vision and creates guitar chord progressions from simple text prompts.

Tell us: *"Nostalgic yet fierce rock chord progression for E Minor"*

We give you:
- Complete chord progression that fits your vibe
- Strumming patterns that match the mood
- Visual chord diagrams
- Audio playback

No music theory degree required. Just describe the sound in your head.

---

## Tech Stack

**Frontend**: React Native, Expo, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS
**Backend**: Node.js, Express
**ML**: Custom-trained Markov Chain / LSTM models
**Database**: InstantDB
